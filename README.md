If you have built an AI product, the hard part is not finding directories. It is finding out,
without creating five accounts, which ones accept open submissions, which ones are curated,
and which ones want paying. This table answers those two questions for every entry.

Where the terms could not be confirmed on the directory's own site, the cell says `Unknown`
rather than a guess. That is a deliberate choice: a wrong `Free` costs somebody an afternoon
and possibly a submission fee, and there are enough entries here that guessing would have
been easy and quietly useless.

Maintained by [TiorAI](https://tiorai.com/), which runs one of the directories listed below.

## How directories are selected

A directory is listed when it meets all three of these:

- It lists products other people made, and accepts submissions or nominations.
- It is currently maintained, with listings added within the past few months.
- Its submission terms can be established from its own site, without an account.

What is excluded, and why the list is shorter than it could have been: link farms that exist
to sell backlinks, directories whose catalogue is scraped wholesale from larger ones, sites
with no submission route, and paid placement presented as editorial selection. A great many
AI directories fail at least one of those.

### How the columns are filled

**Focus** is what the directory is actually about, in a few words.

**Submission** is one of:

- `Open` — anyone can submit and the listing appears, subject to basic checks.
- `Curated` — submissions are reviewed and can be declined on editorial grounds.
- `Invite` — listings are chosen by the operator; there is no public submission route.
- `Unknown` — could not be confirmed on the directory's own site.

**Pricing** is one of:

- `Free` — a free submission route exists and is the normal path.
- `Paid` — submission requires payment.
- `Free/Paid` — a free route exists alongside a paid one that skips the queue or adds placement.
- `Unknown` — could not be confirmed on the directory's own site.

`Unknown` is used wherever the terms were not stated plainly, and it is used often. Guessing
would have filled the table and made it worthless. If you can confirm one of these, the
broken-link form takes corrections and it is the most valuable contribution this repository
can receive.

### About TiorAI's own entry

TiorAI maintains this repository and also runs one of the directories in it. It appears once,
in its alphabetical position, with the same columns filled in by the same rules. It is not
ranked first, not marked as recommended, and not given a longer description.

Entries are ordered alphabetically inside each category. Position carries no meaning, and
there is no paid placement, sponsorship, or affiliate link anywhere in this repository.

Corrections to a `Submission` or `Pricing` cell are the most useful contribution here,
particularly turning an `Unknown` into a confirmed value with a link to where it says so.

If you operate a directory you are suggesting, say so in the form. It will not count against
the submission, and it often means you can confirm the columns better than anyone else.

## Contributing

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the row format and the controlled values. The
one rule with no exceptions: do not guess a `Submission` or `Pricing` value. `Unknown` is
always the correct answer when you have not seen it stated.

## Disclaimer

Every directory listed is operated by a third party. Submission terms, review times, and
pricing change often and without notice, and several of these sites have changed from free to
paid in the past year. Confirm the current terms on the directory's own site before you
submit anything or pay for anything.

Listing here is editorial selection, not endorsement, and it is not a recommendation to submit
to any particular directory. Some listings are worth far more than others, and this table
deliberately does not rank them.
